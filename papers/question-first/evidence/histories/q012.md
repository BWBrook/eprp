**What the history shows (inference; amended per Codex's case-history
review, 76/2167).**
- **Record count.** At `d566f53`, 33 ledger rows have an entry starting
  "Q-012" (34 counting the grouped DC-096). They are extraction records,
  not independent decisions, and most are not yet verified.
- **How the changes came.** Several documented corrections came from the
  second agent (the denominator is not yet verified, Codex 76/2195), through
  computations, exact counterexamples and source checks. Some were Claude's
  own diagnoses: E8, the convergence floor at state 9707, disclosed before
  staging; and E11, the skewed-domain failure of the oracle. That is
  evidence against an over-simple two-agent story.
- **Claude's recurring error type:** stretching a correct observation into
  a broader claim. Examples: causality from saved prefixes; identification
  from geometry; quantities differenced against the wrong reference.
- **The pilot was adaptive development, not one prospective experiment.**
  - **P11 was attempted three times:** the original (`17d129a`), the
    amended attempt (`bba32b2`) and the final Q80 attempt (`bd84335`).
    Each followed disclosed repairs and prospective permissions.
  - **The reference and convergence rules were amended after failures,**
    and the failed originals were preserved.
  - **The narrower supported result:** the final specified attempt failed
    its frozen screen, and sampler fitting stopped there, with no retry and
    no relaxed screen.
  - The laptop interruptions on 21–22 October were operational events, not
    results.
- **No estimator has been chosen.** Q-012 has reached a common
  collapsed-likelihood evaluator and its numerical oracle (E10, E11).
  Maximum marginal likelihood, penalised likelihood and collapsed Bayes
  remain separate possible procedures.
- **Agent time is not instrumented.**
