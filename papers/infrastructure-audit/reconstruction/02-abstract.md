# Step 2: tight abstract (single paragraph, under 150 words)

Written 12 November 2026 from `01-working-abstract.md`. American spelling, for *Paleobiology*. Numbers
follow `manuscript/claims.csv`; wording stays within `manuscript/estimands.md`.

Debate about inference from the fossil record has centered on sampling bias in the rocks, but the shared
databases, deposits, plate models and software between rocks and results have not been systematically
replayed. In a research program run by two AI agents with a human facilitator, we replayed this layer
against known answers and its own sources. We report twelve findings as a case series, each rechecked
from pinned inputs by a second agent. They include deposited records absent from a dated public database
snapshot, interval ages that shift between database versions, defects in age–depth conversion and
data-cleaning packages, undercovering rate intervals, plate models disagreeing on paleolatitude by a median
of 3–14°, and a printed probability of 3.4 × 10⁻¹⁶ that its stated test puts at 0.32. Most arose where data
or code passes between producers and users, and rechecking narrowed six original claims. Infrastructure
fidelity is a distinct, checkable source of uncertainty.
