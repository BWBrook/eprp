# Leads for the question registers

Capabilities found while cataloguing software that bear on register
questions or suggest new ones. The registers belong to the main EPRP agents;
leads reach them as a tranche note on the board, at Barry's direction.
Each lead is labelled `checked` (we read the primary documentation, metadata
or code, or ran the tool), `source claim` (a scan read the paper's text) or
`unverified` (a scan's finding not yet checked here). Question tags are the
scans' inference unless stated.

| L-021 | B-007, Q-013, Q-017, Q-034, Q-041 | **Hiatuses near an extinction degrade the standard range-end bounds.** With six 2-Myr hiatuses in 30 Myr (40% of the time), Strauss–Sadler 95% bounds covered the true extinction 94% of the time away from hiatuses but 83% when the extinction fell inside a hiatus or within 1 Myr after one. The same species without the hiatuses score 92–94% in those classes, so the paired effect is about −9 to −10 points; Solow 1993 (post hoc reading) falls about as far, Robson–Whitlock less. Under homogeneous sampling, and with 0.25-Myr dating error, Strauss–Sadler and Robson–Whitlock are calibrated, while EDE's OLE interval covers only 55–61% (42% with 3–5 fossils).
