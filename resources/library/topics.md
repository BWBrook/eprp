# EPRP canon: topic tree

Ids are `top-level/subtopic`. Every row of `canon.tsv` carries exactly one id; cross-topic relevance is noted in its `why` column. EPRP question numbers (Q-nnn, B-nnn) in brackets mark where a node most directly serves the register.

## roots — Historical roots (pre-1970) (23 works)
- `roots/19c` — (10) Nineteenth- and early twentieth-century foundations: extinction as fact, the first diversity curves, adaptive radiation, branching-process statistics, form and growth.
- `roots/synthesis` — (13) The Simpson-era synthesis and its contemporaries: rates, tempo and mode, periodicity, chronofaunas, equilibrium theory, the allopatric model, Red Queen origin.

## diversity — Diversity dynamics and sampling standardisation [Q-039, Q-040, Q-044, Q-045] (32 works)
- `diversity/curves` — (21) Global and regional Phanerozoic diversity curves, evolutionary faunas, kinetic models of the family and genus record, and the disputed shape of the curve.
- `diversity/sampling` — (11) Rarefaction, coverage and quorum subsampling, per-capita rate estimators, and rock-record and spatial sampling bias.

## extinction — Extinction (mass, background, selectivity, periodicity, causes) [Q-002, Q-004, Q-011, Q-012, Q-016, Q-038, Q-041, Q-042] (55 works)
- `extinction/mass` — (12) The Big Five, magnitude and severity, regime-change arguments, standard syntheses.
- `extinction/causes` — (15) Event-specific causation and timing: impact, large igneous provinces, anoxia, temperature, high-precision geochronology of extinctions, Earth-system recovery.
- `extinction/background` — (9) Background rates, survivorship, age-dependence and the Red Queen test, quiescence and pulsed extinction.
- `extinction/selectivity` — (13) Trait, range, ecology and environment as predictors of extinction risk, background versus mass-extinction regimes, phylogenetic clustering of risk.
- `extinction/periodicity` — (6) Periodicity claims, their statistical critiques and rebuttals, and the astronomical hypotheses.

## origination — Origination, radiation and recovery [Q-007, Q-008, Q-023, Q-033, Q-050, B-001, B-009] (26 works)
- `origination/radiation` — (17) Cambrian and Ordovician radiations, adaptive radiation and ecological opportunity, escalation-era novelties, Ediacaran-Cambrian transition, major clade radiations.
- `origination/recovery` — (9) Post-extinction recovery, lag times, Lazarus taxa, survival and recovery intervals, morphological and ecological rebound.

## rates — Macroevolutionary rate models and birth-death theory [Q-006, Q-019] (30 works)
- `rates/birth-death` — (20) Birth-death theory, reconstructed trees, identifiability, rate shifts, trait-dependent diversification, diversity-dependent models, fossils-versus-phylogenies.
- `rates/fossil-estimation` — (6) Rate estimation directly from occurrence data (PyRate and kin), preservation-aware duration estimators.
- `rates/rates-of-evolution` — (4) Rates of phenotypic evolution, timescale dependence, early-burst and Brownian-rate models.

## phylo — Fossils in phylogenetics [Q-006] (22 works)
- `phylo/stratigraphy` — (5) Stratigraphic congruence, stratocladistics, the role of fossils in cladistics.
- `phylo/morphology-models` — (3) Likelihood models for discrete morphology and the parsimony-versus-Bayesian debate.
- `phylo/dating` — (10) Tip-dating, total-evidence dating, the fossilised birth-death process, fossil calibration practice and software.
- `phylo/traits` — (4) Comparative methods for trait evolution, integrating fossils.

## morpho — Morphospace, disparity and theoretical morphology [Q-050, B-013] (22 works)
- `morpho/theoretical` — (3) Coiling models, theoretical morphospace, constructional morphology, the adaptationist debate.
- `morpho/disparity` — (10) Disparity metrics, Cambrian disparity, decoupling of disparity and diversity, early-burst clades.
- `morpho/evolvability` — (7) Quantitative genetics on macroevolutionary scales, G-matrices, evolvability, integration and modularity, novelty.
- `morpho/methods` — (2) Geometric morphometrics and its software.

## tempo — Trait and lineage time-series: stasis, punctuation, trends [Q-031, Q-036, B-017] (22 works)
- `tempo/punctuated` — (6) Punctuated equilibrium and gradualism, classic fossil lineages, the tempo-and-mode debate.
- `tempo/stasis` — (8) Model-based analysis of fossil time series (paleoTS lineage), stasis and its explanations (adaptive peaks, niche conservatism).
- `tempo/trends` — (8) Cope's rule, driven versus passive trends, body-size evolution, Lilliput effect.

## ecology — Palaeoecology, ecospace and food webs [Q-009, Q-018, Q-021, Q-035, B-001, B-003, B-010] (23 works)
- `ecology/communities` — (9) Coordinated stasis, community persistence and assembly, onshore-offshore patterns, evolutionary faunas as ecological units.
- `ecology/ecospace` — (4) Ecospace utilisation, ecological severity, energy and productivity through time, predation versus competition.
- `ecology/foodwebs` — (5) Fossil food webs, network analyses of extinction and recovery, plant-insect associations, insect diversification and trophic restructuring.
- `ecology/plants` — (5) Plant and terrestrial-flora macroevolution and extinction, angiosperm radiation.

## biogeography — Palaeobiogeography and latitudinal gradients [Q-008, Q-020, Q-027, Q-029, Q-047] (25 works)
- `biogeography/gradients` — (10) Latitudinal diversity gradients through time, out-of-the-tropics, metabolic and climatic explanations.
- `biogeography/provinces` — (4) Provinciality, plate-tectonic control of diversity, palaeogeographic and plate models.
- `biogeography/ranges` — (4) Geographic range as a macroevolutionary property: occupancy, range-duration, range and speciation.
- `biogeography/interchange` — (5) Biotic interchange, Great American Biotic Interchange, invasion in deep time.
- `biogeography/methods` — (2) Likelihood and Bayesian models of range evolution, fossils in biogeographic inference.

## climate — Palaeoclimate-biota coupling and Earth-system models [Q-001, Q-003, Q-015, Q-019, Q-021, Q-049, B-018] (30 works)
- `climate/proxies` — (8) Temperature, CO2, isotope and sea-level reference records.
- `climate/oxygen-carbon` — (6) Oxygen and carbon-cycle models (GEOCARB, COPSE), ocean redox and acidification, thresholds.
- `climate/biota` — (7) Statistical and mechanistic links between climate, temperature, metabolism and diversity or turnover.
- `climate/volcanism` — (4) Large igneous provinces, flood basalts and extinction.
- `climate/hyperthermals` — (5) PETM and other hyperthermals, oceanic anoxic events.

## taphonomy — Taphonomy, completeness and stratigraphic confidence intervals [Q-005, Q-017, Q-042, B-007] (16 works)
- `taphonomy/completeness` — (8) Fossil-record quality, time averaging, preservation heterogeneity, stratigraphic architecture and completeness.
- `taphonomy/preservation` — (2) Exceptional preservation and decay.
- `taphonomy/ranges` — (6) Signor-Lipps, confidence intervals on stratigraphic ranges, Bayesian range-end estimators.

## time — Geochronology and time-scale uncertainty [Q-041] (10 works)
- `time/scale` — (3) The Geologic Time Scale and chronostratigraphic charts.
- `time/radiometric` — (5) U-Pb, Ar-Ar and astronomical calibration, EARTHTIME, cyclostratigraphy.
- `time/correlation` — (2) Graphic correlation, constrained optimisation, quantitative biostratigraphy, appearance-event ordination.

## theory — Theoretical models [Q-010, Q-011, Q-024, Q-030] (32 works)
- `theory/red-queen` — (5) Red Queen, Court Jester, incumbency and biotic versus abiotic drivers.
- `theory/logistic-kinetic` — (8) Logistic, kinetic and equilibrium diversity models; bounded versus unbounded richness; diversity dependence.
- `theory/stochastic` — (3) Stochastic clade models, null models, the nomothetic programme.
- `theory/selection` — (5) Species selection, hierarchy theory and sorting.
- `theory/criticality` — (3) Self-organised criticality and extinction-size statistics.
- `theory/early-warning` — (2) Early-warning indicators for critical transitions and tipping elements.
- `theory/neutral` — (2) Neutral theory and its deep-time extensions.
- `theory/escalation` — (4) Escalation, the Mesozoic marine revolution, predation through time.

## data — Data infrastructures and tools [Q-040, Q-045] (13 works)
- `data/databases` — (8) Sepkoski compendia, PBDB, Macrostrat, Neptune/Triton lineage, mammal and trait databases.
- `data/tools` — (5) Statistical software, R packages and text-mining systems used for fossil data.

## conservation — Conservation palaeobiology and Quaternary megafaunal extinctions [Q-034, Q-037] (24 works)
- `conservation/baselines` — (7) Baselines, death assemblages, Quaternary ecology, restoration and policy.
- `conservation/megafauna` — (13) Late Quaternary megafaunal extinctions, humans versus climate, ecosystem consequences.
- `conservation/islands` — (4) Island and Holocene extinctions, human-contact extinctions.

## crisis — Deep-time versus modern crisis comparisons [Q-013, Q-022, Q-025, Q-028, Q-048] (23 works)
- `crisis/comparisons` — (18) The sixth mass extinction debate, background-rate baselines, fossil-calibrated risk, rate comparisons for warming and carbon release.
- `crisis/phylogenetic-loss` — (5) Loss of evolutionary history, extinction risk classification, recovery times.
