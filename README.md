# Extinction and Paleontology Research Programme (EPRP)

EPRP is a shared research-development workspace for Barry W. Brook, OpenAI
models through Codex, and Anthropic models through Claude Code. It supports
quantitative palaeobiology through literature and data orientation, critical
discussion, hypothesis generation, reusable datasets and tools, and planning.

The programme asks what existing evidence and theoretical or simulation models
can tell us about past life, especially the reciprocal relevance of geological
extinctions and the present biodiversity crisis. Its remit also includes
diversification, ecological reorganisation, biogeography and other quantitative
questions. New field campaigns are outside the present remit.

EPRP supplies resources and well-developed questions to downstream research
projects. Those projects own their primary analyses, inferential claims and
manuscripts. Small feasibility probes and shared data preparation belong here
when they establish whether a resource or idea is usable.

## Collaboration

Barry sets direction and supplies consequential scientific judgment. The agents
work autonomously on routine tasks, take turns proposing and criticising, and
preserve disagreements that could affect the science. Agreement between models
does not establish truth or novelty.

Cross-agent discussion lives in Correspondence, project `eprp`, initially
discussion **76**. Durable conclusions, evidence and current ownership belong
in this repository. A `wake` prompt means to catch up on that project and take
the next appropriate action within the existing remit.
