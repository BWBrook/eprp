# B-002 held-source feasibility tranche

30 October 2026. Authorised by Barry's instruction quoted in Correspondence
96/2582, following Claude's critique in 96/2581. Main Codex owns this directory.
This checks whether the proposed empirical coupling and physical calculations
can identify a buoyancy-route comparison. It does not estimate evolutionary
transition rates or select a primary project.

The primary-source audit and workbook schema are already known. The following
small descriptive calculation is declared before running it; it is not a
confirmatory biological test or a representative species survey.

## Workbook calculation

- Input: the unchanged Walton–Korn workbook, SHA256
  `fbe2012f1d420ef7b292141ae0ed0ea8d046c4c15303663565dc72e0727ebe40`.
- Keep original rows, including repeated taxon labels; no inferred specimen
  identity, maturity, relationship or source correction. Retain original family
  and source labels only as descriptions.
- Compare ratios at native diameter values **2 versus 10**, and **5 versus 20**.
  These are analyst-chosen size references, not juvenile/adult assignments.
  The workbook's diameter column is not explicitly unit-labelled. Use its
  native numbers without adding units to raw records.
- Require all three ratios at each retained measurement position. Sort by
  diameter only after verifying that numeric diameters in original whorl order
  strictly decrease. Do not interpolate a nonmonotone row.
- Linearly interpolate each ratio in log diameter between joint observations;
  require bracket diameter ratio at most two. Preserve bracket cell locators
  and weights. No extrapolation, zero replacement, maturity imputation or
  ratio reconstruction from absent raw dimensions.
- For each reference pair, report eligible rows/families and the fitted
  in-sample R² for each early ratio predicted by the three late ratios with
  an intercept. Also report same-ratio Pearson correlation. Fit one row per
  source series. These are descriptions of this selected sample: no p-values,
  inferential confidence intervals, independent-replicate claims or threshold
  classifying developmental coupling.
- Compare with the source's late-shape inclusion rules. The selected sample,
  unknown maturity and unmeasured hereditary variation preclude using either
  low or high association as a route-connectivity decision.

## Physical and mathematical checks

Inspect original Naglik and Tajika tables to distinguish volumes, thicknesses,
surface areas, reconstructed endpoint masses and through-growth observations.
An endpoint mass-balance replay or volume-proportion diagnostic may be made
from supplied quantities, retaining reconstruction assumptions. A thickness
fit or stagewise loading calculation requires its actual missing observables;
neither follows merely from a volume table.

Check the strict route certificate, quasi-concavity conditions, decoupling
argument and grid-path certification. Preserve counterexamples that change
the proposed stopping rules. A non-significant allometry is not an equivalence
test. A valid all-stage path can refute an obstruction for its fixed endpoints
and scenario; a missed path in a discretisation does not prove obstruction.

Stop at a source-supported physical comparison or an exact support-limit
result. Do not invent a programme family from correlations, merge different
specimens into an animal, or commission a route engine without the physical
map it would evaluate. Q-009's source-owner support request is separate and
pending acceptance; it is not automatic promotion if this tranche closes.
