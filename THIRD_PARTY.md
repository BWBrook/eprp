## Verbatim question texts (`resources/library/notes/question-agendas/`)

`questions.tsv` quotes research questions word for word from published horizon scans, reviews and agendas, with the source recorded in `sources.tsv`. The text of a question is held only where the source is open (`text_in_repo` = yes in `sources.tsv`); items from sources with no open licence have an empty `text` field.

Most of those sources are CC BY (664 items) or public-domain, which the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence of this repository accommodates with attribution. **245 items (244 from CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-ND sources, 1 from a CC BY-NC-SA source) come from sources under non-commercial licences, and are not covered by this repository's CC BY-SA 4.0 grant.** They stay under their original terms:

- CC BY-NC (attribution, non-commercial): 97 items;
- CC BY-NC-ND (attribution, non-commercial, no derivatives): 147 items, reproduced as short verbatim quotations with attribution and not adapted;
- CC BY-NC-SA (attribution, non-commercial, share-alike): 1 item.

Do not reuse these question texts commercially, and do not treat them as licensed for adaptation. The wording is quoted for scholarly comment and indexing. To reuse a question commercially, obtain it from the source. A reader who wants a fully open copy can drop the `text` column for the rows below, or filter on `licence` in `sources.tsv`. The `source_id`, `item`, `section`, `form` and `themes` fields are the project's own annotation. Items from CC BY-SA sources (12) carry the same ShareAlike condition as this repository.

## Other bundled third-party material

- **`resources/library/notes/big-questions-2025/`**: tables derived from Smith et al. (2025), *Paleobiology*, published under CC BY 4.0. Attribute that paper.
- **`resources/library/works.tsv` and the other bibliography tables**: bibliographic metadata (identifiers, titles, years, venues, citation counts, open-access status) from OpenAlex, Crossref and similar open sources. They hold no abstracts, full text or PDFs.
- **`resources/sources/**/profile/` and similar folders**: aggregate counts and profiles that the project computed from public databases (for example the Paleobiology Database, Neotoma, Macrostrat, Fossilworks and OCTOPUS layers). The upstream databases keep their own licences, recorded in each source's README, and their raw downloads are not in this repository.
- **`resources/sources/fossilworks/profile/`**: derived evidence tables from the Fossilworks Ecological Register, whose data licence is stated as CC0 by the Fossilworks FAQ. The Fossilworks website and software are separately copyrighted, and the saved HTML response pages under `reference-recovery-*` are kept as short provenance receipts of public bibliographic records, not as a mirror.
- **`docs/review/`**: texts returned by external AI systems (OpenAI GPT-6 Pro) in response to prompts that the project wrote. They are attributed in each file.
- **Published works discussed in notes**: short quotations with citation, for criticism and review. Their copyright stays with the authors and publishers.
