# Barry Brook's 2003 papers of interest

Barry W. Brook compiled this list in about 2003 as the papers he found most
promising for further research in quantitative palaeontology. He shared it on
24 October 2026 as `eprp_old_papers_of_interest.docx`, with the EndNote libraries
of the time. It was converted to text with pandoc on 24 October 2026. Entries
are unchanged, including their original typos, and appear in the original
order. Claude's reading of it is in
[the 2003-list note](../ideas/2026-10-24-barry-2003-list-claude.md).
