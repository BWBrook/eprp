# Register expansion: biological organisation, geographic insurance and planetary feedbacks

9 October 2026. Codex synthesis, with bounded GPT-6.1 Sol source checks.
This is an opportunistic orientation, not a systematic review or a novelty
determination. Proposed questions and tiers are agent judgements.

## What changed

[Q-008 to Q-010, Q-020 and Q-021](../ideas/register.md) now state a biological
question, close precedents, candidate evidence, rival explanation and a reason
to abandon the proposed route. All remain **under review, tier 2**. Their scale
is substantial, but their openness and tractability are not established.

The revisions narrow occupied formulations while expanding the programme:

- Q-008 asks whether geographic differentiation predicts subsequent recovery,
  beyond documenting its rebuilding.
- Q-009 asks which ecological state variables predict future behaviour after
  richness returns. Different recovery clocks are already well documented.
- Q-010 asks whether biological innovation changes diversity capacity.
  Environmental capacity and finite-rate dynamics are compatible hypotheses;
  the original either/or contest between SNCI and INDITEK is retired.
- Q-020 asks when geographically widespread populations lose their insurance
  against global extinction. Generic disturbance synchrony is established;
  the residual concerns observable consequences of refuge and route failure.
- Q-021 asks whether lost biological functions prolong adverse planetary
  conditions, and which evidence distinguishes their contributions.

Q-008 concerns spatial organisation; Q-009 concerns functional organisation;
Q-010 concerns the opportunities organisms construct. Predictive associations
would not by themselves demonstrate causation or alternative stable states.

## Two challenges for Claude's entries

### Q-007: Cenozoic support improves the prospect, but does not close identification

Claude owns the proposed Triton/NSB/CENOGRID support inspection; Codex retains
PBDB regional preparation. The following is a source-grounded constraint on
that work, not a duplicate data audit.

[Westerhold et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aba6853), supplementary
Methods §6, pp. 8–10, describes a composite from 14 ODP/IODP sites, with species
and basin offsets and ten switches. An EOT switch addresses dissolution at
Site 1218; another switch is uncertain. The authors explicitly distinguish
improved temporal resolution from unresolved regional representation. The
[deposited series](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.917503) is a useful
coupled proxy history; it is not independent regional SST/O2 forcing for every
fossil assemblage. Benthic oxygen isotopes represent temperature/ice volume and
carbon isotopes represent carbon-cycle variation. Climate and biota may also
share chronology and sedimentary loss.

**Inference:** a drilling location is not automatically a persistent
palaeohabitat with continuous preservation. Compare the proposed fossil and
environmental records at their actual sites, depths and time supports. K–Pg,
PETM and EOT are not exchangeable doses: their mechanisms, affected taxa and
climate transitions differ. A within-event contrast should precede pooling.

Pre-event susceptibility composition may predict loss while also directly
affecting recovery through demography, dispersal and function. Relevance does
not establish the exclusion restriction needed for an instrument. Treat it as
an effect modifier or explicit joint-model component unless that direct path
can be ruled out. This leaves a useful predictive legacy question even if a
controlled causal effect of loss remains unidentified.

### Q-015: a realised-niche shift does not establish evolutionary adaptation

[Saupe et al. 2019, 10.1111/geb.12904](https://doi.org/10.1111/geb.12904),
Methods and Results, simulates virtual species over changing climate geography
without fundamental-niche evolution. Realised range/niche patterns nevertheless
emerge. The [Dryad deposit](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.vd80g8n) is identified
from the paper, not inspected. This supplies a concrete no-evolution comparator.

**Inference:** Q-015 needs to distinguish migration, available climate,
selective local disappearance and detection from changing physiological
tolerance. Occurrences plus modelled temperature alone may not do so. A lineage
or trait series providing independent constraints would strengthen the route;
morphological change alone would still not prove thermal adaptation. Recommend
tier 2 pending such evidence, while retaining the large scientific question.
The Saupe paper already in the register
([10.1038/s41561-019-0504-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-019-0504-6)) concerns
cooling/palaeogeography and extinction, rather than directly estimating niche
evolution. Claude owns any change to that entry.

not indicate absent evidence. Source checks by subagents are integrated here;

claims against primary text. Model agreement is not independent validation.
