## CN1 — Does specialisation erode the ability to restore an essential function?

**Question and claim.** Can a long period of dependence on other organisms
reduce recovery after provider interruption even when present service output
and the measured abundance of alternatives are similar? The proposed mechanism
is loss of autonomous competence or accessible restorative pathways during
specialisation. The target is the probability of restoring a specified
function before dependants fail, under a specified disturbance and environmental
path. Prediction from dependence history and identification of that mechanism
are separate claims.

**Why the answer matters.** A community could maintain richness and current
function while losing the ability to reorganise. Conversely, apparently
dependent communities might recover rapidly from rare retained alternatives.
Distinguishing these outcomes would change what functional resilience means.

**Strongest rival.** Alternatives remain competent but are competitively
suppressed or scarce. Provider removal releases them; no capability has eroded.
Immigration, environmental differences, changing demand and different times to
failure are additional explanations that cannot be assigned to erosion through
a residual history coefficient.

**Discriminating result.** In a generative comparison, match the environmental
path, present function, alternative abundance, import and failure criterion,
then preserve or restore competence while leaving pre-interruption competition
unchanged. A distinct recovery effect would locate the mechanism in competence
or pathway availability. An empirical application would need measurements that
can separate these states; failure to distinguish them is an identification
result, not evidence for erosion.

**Nearest known prior and register overlap.** Meijer, van Dijk & Hogeweg
(2020), [10.1038/s42003-020-1107-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-020-1107-x),
already models dependent communities and rescue by standing autonomous
variants. Black Queen theory occupies adaptive functional loss. Q-007/Q-009
already cover historical effects and ecological state; a new contract must
explain its additional mechanism or prediction. The
[dependence note](2026-10-24-dependence-information-and-new-opportunities-codex.md)
records the primary-source checks and the withdrawn coral argument.

**First bounded check.** Compare those mechanistic priors against a minimal
provider–dependant–alternative model, specifying competence separately from
abundance and service delivery. Determine whether the proposed distinction is
already generated and analysed by the existing models. Treat foraminiferal
isotope evidence as a separate observation check, not a measurement of partner
continuity or dependence duration by assumption.

**Retire or merge if.** The contribution is just “history predicts outcomes,”
or an existing model already establishes the same recoverability boundary.
Likewise, changing an unobserved parameter to make recovery worse is not by
itself a scientific advance. Retain a new contract only for a consequential
and distinguishable mechanism, prediction or synthesis.

## CN2 — Can changing cue reliability reverse which response strategies persist?

**Question and claim.** Holding the environmental stress process and
physiological tolerance fixed, can a change in the association between cues
and future conditions reverse the relative long-term performance or persistence
of response strategies? The mechanism is a changed predictive relationship
at the relevant response lag, not a change in sensing accuracy. The hypothesis
must connect phenotype mismatch to demographic consequences; mismatch alone
is not an extinction outcome.

**Why the answer matters.** Evolutionary selectivity may depend on how organisms
anticipate conditions as well as how well they tolerate them. This retains the
sensory-access branch beyond predator escalation and develops F1's question
about temporal environmental structure.

**Strongest rival.** Conventional physiology, demographic delay or costs of
plasticity explain the strategy ranking. Cue dependence contributes no
additional result once these mechanisms are represented. Direct sampling,
rapid retuning and diversified responses may remove or reverse the prediction.

**Discriminating result.** Keep the environmental process, its autocorrelation,
the fitness function and response costs fixed, while varying the cue–future-
environment association within a coherent joint process. Compare cue following
with direct or delayed sampling and a diversified or fixed response. Ask whether
the rankings differ over defined time horizons, rather than assume that slow
generation time equals long prediction horizon.

**Nearest known prior and register overlap.** Chevin & Lande (2015),
[10.1111/evo.12755](https://doi.org/10.1111/evo.12755), already models multiple
correlated cues; only its abstract was checked in the ideation round. Evolutionary
rescue, plasticity and environmental-predictability theory are therefore central
prior targets. Q-028 concerns trait-dependent success across crises; Q-031 is
specifically size change under warming and is only adjacent. F1, H3 and the
life-history seed provide the connection, without reopening Q-038 or Q-012.

**First bounded check.** Read the closest cue/plasticity and evolutionary-rescue
models in full and construct a comparison of their state variables, stochastic
environment, response lag and demographic target. Establish whether a
nontrivial contribution remains beyond the already checked binary mismatch
example. Any empirical route then needs a separate observation model for cue,
target and response; seasonal extremes are not cue correlation.

**Retire or merge if.** Existing theory already supplies the same reversal and
demographic conditions, or the proposed result follows solely from comparing a
good predictor with a bad one. A deep-time illustration alone would not cure
that lack of contribution. A supported residual could still be theoretical;
an immediate fossil measurement is not required for that claim.

## CN3 — Does the persistence of enabling conditions control which innovations establish?

**Question and claim.** Can innovations fail to establish because biological
and environmental prerequisites do not remain jointly available long enough,
despite repeated origin of the relevant trait and ample total opportunity?
The specific target is establishment probability and delay when total jointly
suitable time and innovation supply are matched, but its continuity or timing
relative to development and construction differs. This develops the earlier
innovation-window branch, rather than treating delayed radiation as evidence
of absent innovation.

**Why the answer matters.** The conditions allowing a form to originate may
differ from those allowing it to become a persistent lineage or assemblage.
That distinction bears on failed innovations, delayed radiations and losses
of evolutionary opportunity while component lineages remain.

**Strongest rival.** Standard demographic growth, extinction during unsuitable
intervals, rare innovation supply or incumbency explains the delay. No special
developmental or ecological prerequisite is needed. Apparent first appearances
could also be delayed by observation rather than establishment.

**Discriminating result.** Compare a model with an explicit developmental,
partner-assembly or habitat-construction requirement against a simple
establishment model, matching innovation supply, background growth, disturbance
and total joint suitability. Permute the duration and alignment of the enabling
episodes. The contribution must be a specific prediction beyond ordinary
stochastic establishment; the fact that simultaneous prerequisites must coincide
is not sufficient.

**Nearest known prior and register overlap.** The
[innovation-window scenario](2026-10-24-blue-sky-connections-codex.md)
and H1 are exploratory, with no completed nearest-prior brief. Ecological
opportunity, incumbency, community assembly and establishment in fluctuating
environments are the first prior families to check. B-002 addresses juvenile
viability of otherwise useful adult forms; B-013 addresses disparity–diversity
lags; B-001 addresses reassembly. This card must retain a distinct target or
be developed within one of those contracts.

**First bounded check.** Map those prior mechanisms to a two-stage origin/
establishment model and identify an explicit requirement with an independently
specified duration or renewal process. Before choosing a fossil case, determine
whether the supposed prerequisite adds a result beyond existing establishment
theory. If historical application is retained, specify how trait origin,
establishment and first preserved appearance could be distinguished.

**Retire or merge if.** The result reduces to an overlap count, generic
demographic delay, or a restatement of B-002/B-013. The branch is worth a
bounded formalisation check, not an assumed claim that ecological windows
explain particular radiations.

## Position of these nominations

CN1 and CN2 are my preferred first pair because they preserve two distinct
mechanisms and can be challenged using explicit models without premature fossil
claims. CN3 keeps an earlier, less recently elaborated possibility in the union.
Co-transmission remains an important rival and a candidate in its own right;
developmental induction remains separate from predictive cue use. Neither is
rejected by omission from my three cards. The closing map preserves the wider
portfolio. Comparison with Claude's cards and accepted ownership come next.
