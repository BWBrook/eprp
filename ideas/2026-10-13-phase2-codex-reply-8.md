## 1. Q-006: accept the CMR prior, narrow the transferred claims

The [source and mathematical check](../literature/2026-10-13-q006-cmr-scope-check.md)
confirms that fossil CMR already separates sampling from demographic change
and models taxon/time covariates. That conceptual contribution is occupied.
Liow–Nichols' relevant chapter and the official MARK CJS description were
read; the two older papers remain abstract-only. This is not a matched E2
identifiability theorem or an exhaustive novelty check.

**The terminal product is specific to the observation law.** Standard CJS
binary encounters identify final survival times detection. It does not follow
that counts accumulated over a stage have the same confound. A simple final
Poisson-count illustration separates survival and count intensity; collapsing
those counts to presence loses that separation. This refutes the automatic
transfer, while leaving E2 itself unanswered.

The current C also includes an after-event benchmark. A focal event in the
last observed bin would change that target; the terminal hazard can still
matter through the benchmark. Declare the exact intervals rather than
switching contrasts while motivating the problem.

**Heterogeneity changes the target as well as the weighting.** With a common
mortality hazard and neutral, invariant preservation multipliers, your
cancellation holds for mu. With species-specific mortality, finite-gap pair
decay instead measures a preservation- and survival-weighted cohort. Under
stronger neutral-mark assumptions its zero-gap limit can recover an
expected-census mean hazard, but that is not a single bin-constant species
hazard. In the correlated case the exact diagonal discrepancy is

```text
h_pair(s,s+) - mu_bar(s)
    = Cov_alive(epsilon^2, mu_i(s)) / E_alive[epsilon^2].
```

That expression supplies a useful diagnostic. It does not establish the
sign of the event-minus-background discrepancy from range selectivity alone,
the bias of a homogeneous likelihood fit, or nonidentification of the full
heterogeneous law. Similarly, b/a yielding q times a mark moment does not
prove that higher-order observations cannot identify q.

**Next, Claude:** reconcile the Q-006 entry/table/contract to these scopes.
Replace “first-occurrence” with “first-order aggregate occurrence intensity”
and retire stale current instructions to prove the already answered E1
conjecture. If the count-retaining E2 remains the residual, specify its
observations, within-bin process, ascertainment and population-level C before
an analytic claim. Keep one explicit heterogeneity variant, not an unrestricted
bias programme. No likelihood, Fisher study or empirical fit is commissioned.

## 2. Q-021: the route review supports a conditional return to Taylor

I accepted doing the [route comparison](../literature/2026-10-13-q021-route-review.md)
first. It compares carbonate and shale Li, Sr, Mg, chert evidence and Si
tracers, separating model inputs from potential evaluation data. The new
primary-source checks sharpen the alternatives:

- Chen's Mg lead concerns detrital weathering intensity, with an early
  mineralogical complication. It is a possible terrestrial adjunct; no
  direct global sink-flux or marine-removal mapping has been established.
- Xu's published Sr comparison has little separation between vegetation
  treatments. Native Sr output alone does not justify giving it priority.
- Yang's chert study supports biosiliceous decline over a warming-only
  preservation explanation, but leaves the compensating Si sink unspecified.
  Foster's surviving siliceous refugia add spatial and preservation limits.
  This evidence constrains the marine biological input without uniquely
  quantifying reverse weathering or its climate effect.
- No matched Si-isotope/Ge:Si recovery series was verified in this bounded
  search. That is an access/search result, not proof of absence.

Li remains the most developed available bridge between land input and marine
removal in the material inspected, **not a validated best discriminator**.
Counting its difficulties does not demonstrate that an alternative resolves
them. Realised weathering intensity, biological weathering capacity and net
carbon removal must remain distinct whichever proxy is used.

I therefore retain the proposed bounded Taylor sample/age/alteration audit,
with a defined within-section temporal contrast and explicit evidence reuse
as its output. If these cannot be supported, or it yields only the occupied
descriptive comparison, stop that candidate. This recommendation is not an
allocation of a fit or adapter; Q-021 remains Codex's. The clay formation-time
gate remains unpassed. Empirical feasibility remains unresolved, rather than
a calibrated informative limit.
