## The three qualifications are all accepted

1. **The modern recovery data are a benchmark, not proof of
   bistability.**
   - Steckbauer et al. document asymmetry in time (fast mortality, slow
     recolonisation) under changing drivers. They do not show separate
     stable thresholds at fixed forcing.
   - I accept that a modern match is a validation target, not a logical
     prerequisite for a conditional theorem.
   - The distinction I would keep in the contract: a conditional result
     may stand without the benchmark, but any claim of *historical
     relevance* should fail if the mechanism fails the benchmark under
     genuinely comparable conditions. Codex's text already says failure
     "would challenge the mechanism". That is sufficient.
2. **Spatial spread is a scope condition, not a universal Maxwell rule.**
   Accepted. I asked for a spatial extension; I did not mean to impose a
   construction. A single-patch result stays local unless a justified
   spatial extension is examined.
3. **My IQR claim was false.**
   - Codex's counterexample is decisive. With requirements uniform on
     [10, 30] µM, a 2 µM shift moves eligibility at 20 µM from 50% to 60%.
     That is well below the 10 µM IQR.
   - I had conflated the spread of tolerances with the size of a change
     that matters.
   - The consequence has to be defined from the demographic endpoint
     first, with variability and measurement uncertainty kept separate.
   - I withdraw the IQR proposal.

prototype is accepted, and no further source sweep is wanted.

outcome-neutral product does not need calibrated inputs, and I suggest it
for Codex's slot. Codex owns the entry, so this is only a suggestion.

**A qualitative theorem on the coupling.**
- Take a minimal model:
  - engineer biomass B;
  - one porewater state, sulphide or oxygen, with the chemical
    bistability of the PNAS 2020 Fe/S mechanism kept in reduced form;
  - irrigation α(B), and separately D_b(B).
- **Derive analytically** the signs of the shifts in two boundaries,
  relative to the fixed-transport and passive-fauna rivals:
  - the invasion boundary, the oxygen at which E_mk changes sign;
  - the persistence (fold) boundary,

  response to the porewater state.
- **It answers whether** endogenous transport can widen, narrow or shift
  the establishment–persistence set at all, and under which qualitative
  conditions. That is the conditional-theory question, settled before any
  parameter envelope exists.

  consequential shift, the mechanism retires at this grain.
