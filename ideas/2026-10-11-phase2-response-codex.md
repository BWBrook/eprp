the single A/B/C interpretation with two explicit judgements: **scientific

## Rubric changes

1. **Significance concerns the residual, not just the headline.** State what
   would change under either a well-supported positive or negative answer.
   Modern transfer should be a tag, not a bonus inside significance. Deep-time
   evolutionary questions need no present-day application to count as major.
   Q-010 and Q-021 already have S=3: raising significance cannot fix their
   position in the queue. I propose S=3 rather than 2 for Q-026 and Q-050,
   conditional on their specified predictive/mechanistic residuals surviving.
2. **Openness and confidence in the review are different.** O=3, "clearly
   open after a bounded check", overstates what the searches establish.
   Q-017, Q-042 and Q-047 have residuals worth pursuing, with novelty still
   unverified. Record nearest occupied result, proposed residual and review
   depth separately; absence from a scan is not a novelty certificate.
3. **Data readiness must refer to the proposed comparison.** Separate
   source access, usable fields/units, and joint biological/temporal support.
   Deposits can be open while the required crosswalk, independent exposure,
   controls or chronological uncertainty remain unavailable or uninspected.
   Keep licence/version and shared-source dependencies alongside these facts.
4. **Order gates by information gained, then cost.** Name the decision each
   gate could change and what either outcome would mean. Distinguish a cheap
   counterexample, a measurement-resolution test, and a discrimination test
   under empirically defensible rivals. Avoid numerical gate scores that hide
   these differences. Runtime estimates remain provisional until specified.

**Q-049's first gate needs sharpening.** A ratio estimated across the loss

Test predictive performance against the habitat/pathway models, not only
precision of R. A negative coefficient is a scientific hypothesis, not a
condition for accepting a valid analysis. These are deductions from the
current design, not an empirical rejection of tracking effects.

## New evidence: Q-050 covariance support

The [reproducible inspection](../data/q050-covariance-support/README.md)
downloaded and checksum-verified Tsuboi's versioned archive, then inspected
the two Brombacher RData objects with base R. Each has 75 samples and two
traits, on the same stored 474.57-kyr grid. Median spacing is 6.18 kyr.
Numerical dimension/finite-value/covariance checks passed. One series has a
sample of only three specimens. The objects do not contain the explicit
absolute-age, sample-ID, environmental or age-error fields needed for the
joint comparison; the original source remains to be crosswalked.

The README's description of `tt` as a lapse between steps was misleading
for our previous interpretation: these stored values are elapsed coordinates,
as supported by the archive's own checking/analysis code. Spacings are
`diff(tt)`, not `tt`. The audit preserves the raw values and corrects our
earlier time-increment description. It establishes a real numerical starting
point while leaving both model discrimination and novelty unverified.
