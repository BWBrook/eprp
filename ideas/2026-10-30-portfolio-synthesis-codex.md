## B-002: the result the next tranche should earn

The [paper specification](../literature/2026-10-15-b002-paper-specification.md)
distinguishes growth within an organism from change between complete growth
programmes. Under the same reconstruction and allowed routes, let `B_A`
denote the best bottleneck margin with terminal requirements and `B_J` the
best bottleneck with the specified earlier requirements added. Their ordering
`B_J <= B_A` is automatic. It cannot be the scientific result.

A strict obstruction requires **two all-stage viable endpoints, `B_A > 0`
and `B_J < 0`**. A numerical optimiser failing to find a path does not prove
that no path exists. The conclusion is conditional on the declared programme
family and physical scenario; discrete changes may bypass a continuous-route
barrier. An innovation claim additionally needs an independently justified
terminal performance improvement. Without that, the result concerns physical
accessibility, not increased fitness, historical irreversibility or waiting time.

The proposed tranche should carry the comparison through to one inspectable
physical result, or a precise reason that this chosen mechanism cannot support
it. Its internal steps are:

1. **Resolve the closest-prior difference and physical domain together.**
   Use the existing [generator/prior review](../literature/2026-10-15-b002-generator-and-physical-priors.md).
   Urdy already supplies coupled growth; Naglik and Tajika supply volumetric
   and hydrostatic precedents. Bayer 1978 and Urdy's appendices remain the
   named access gaps. Legitimate retrieval can resolve them; unread material
   remains an access limit. Select one physical requirement with a defensible
   stage ecology, linking shell, septum, tissue and fluid quantities to sources.
2. **Establish whether an earlier-stage requirement can bind at all.**
   Under geometric similarity and fixed density/volume fractions, mass and
   displaced volume both scale as length cubed: the neutral-buoyancy loading
   requirement is size invariant. An effect needs a supported departure from
   that null. Use one physically consistent fluid allocation across simultaneous
   constraints. A juvenile must not acquire an adult swimming requirement just
   because that makes a barrier appear.
3. **For a supported binding mechanism, compare routes and challenge the
   restriction.** Freeze the endpoint rule before examining route outcomes.
   Test an explicit expansion of the programme family and supported physiological
   compensation. Preserve coherent reconstruction scenarios across growth.
   Seek a certified exclusion or report a numerical candidate obstruction at
   its actual evidential level. A defensible all-stage bypass rejects a barrier
   for that endpoint pair and scenario, even if the initial restricted search
   appeared persuasive.

The deliverable is a physical input table, one matched terminal/all-stage
comparison, the decisive controls and a disposition. The strongest result
would establish or eliminate the obstruction across a defended range. If
physical inputs cannot support such a comparison, the deliverable is an
explicit support limit and closure of this chosen route. That is a useful
allocation decision, but it is not the biological result sought by a protected
primary-study slot. A broad simulation engine, additional specimen campaign
or repeated review of the already-settled mathematics would not advance this
tranche.

Why put this first? The unresolved object is concrete, its null is sharp, and
its main counterexample can be tested within a modest physical construction.
Q-016's remaining claim is narrower than initially presented; Q-009's more
consequential empirical outcome is still unsupported; Q-012 requires a larger
inference-method commitment. B-002 wins this comparison provisionally on the
value of a bounded discriminating result, not on established novelty or data
readiness. If its physical support fails, return the result to this comparison
without automatically commissioning the next card.

## Empirical alternatives and the Q-012 disagreement

**Q-009.** The [K–Pg alignment](../data/kpg-plankton-alignment/README.md)
establishes source correspondence, preserves missing values and explains the
El Kef exclusion mismatch. The key next evidence is a measured later response,
predictor windows, forcing/environment coverage, age support and valid time
blocks. A later response can use nonoverlapping observations of composition
or a separate process proxy; it need not come from a different measurement
family. It must avoid reusing the predictor information as its apparent
validation. Missing original integer counts limits some count-based models,
but does not prohibit every analysis of the percentages. Site contrasts,
investigator changes and chronology remain substantive rivals. Coordinate
this finite check with the existing References owner rather than duplicating it.

**Q-016.** A concrete smaller target is: in planktonic foraminifera at fixed
one-Myr landmarks, does contraction calculated solely from the past improve
prediction of no recorded appearance in the following one-Myr bin, beyond
current range, observed age and sampling effort? Train on older blocks and
evaluate later ones; retain the range-by-contraction interaction indicated by
the closest prior. Compare paired Brier scores and calibration, with uncertainty
respecting repeated species and temporal dependence. Full-lifespan trimming
cannot be imported into prefix-only predictors. This explicitly forecasts
the record. A biological extinction interpretation requires additional
observation/outcome support. The proposed prior/support brief must check
the closest supplements and code, then count complete histories, outcome
classes, follow-up and evaluation blocks in one fixed archive. It should
establish whether this narrower payoff is both distinct and worth pursuing
before a fit is commissioned.

**B-001.** The [recovery synthesis](../literature/2026-10-28-extinction-recovery-disagreements-codex.md)
identifies a direct historical alternative: compare independently dated Early
Triassic localities and repeated horizons under a common observable role set.
Include ordinary deposits and reported nondetections with their preservation
and effort context. Guiyang already establishes an early complex locality;
a second unlike exceptional deposit is not a new global recovery account.
Repeated detections demonstrate recurrence, with continuous persistence still
requiring additional support. If comparable locality/horizon pairs are absent,
retain a restricted occurrence synthesis and drop prevalence or persistence
claims. The held cross-event B-001 contract and its affected PBDB counts do
not supply evidence for this smaller comparison.

**Q-012.** The [information screen](../data/q012-information-screen/README.md)
establishes complete-data power lower bounds above 0.8 in both fixed-nuisance
ensembles. Its frozen recorded detector reaches only 0.126 and 0.1785 power.
Those results do not bound all recorded-data methods. The
[applicability audit](../data/q012-information-screen/lr-applicability-review.md)
also shows that P11 omits hidden lineages and ancestry reconstruction required
by the actual observation map. Claude is right that an exact recorded-data
likelihood ratio is optimal for the specified simple comparison. The further
claim that restarting the queued sampler is the only useful route is not
established. Approximate or simulation-based tests could demonstrate useful
recorded-data discrimination; their failure would not establish an information
ceiling. A correct likelihood method would still need numerical validation
and empirical applicability. This is a substantive allocation disagreement,
not a declaration that inheritance is absent or inference impossible.
