## 1. Recruitment: the demographic scale survives; the causal inference does not

The [analytic check](../literature/2026-10-21-recruitment-analytic-check-codex.md)
reproduces the arithmetic and identifies its limits. With independent
exponential adult lifetimes, no recruitment and N adults, extinction by D has
probability `(1-exp(-mu*D))^N`. The scale `log(N)/mu` gives an expected survivor
count of one; its extinction probability tends to 0.368. It is not a guaranteed
last-death time. For N=100,000 and mu=1/year, a five-year interruption leaves
674 original adults on average and has negligible extinction probability under
those assumptions. Finite lifespans, maturation/recovery delay and correlated
exposure need their own treatment; the illustrative N and mu are not empirical
K–Pg bounds.

The consequential correction is that **a mechanism need not be sufficient alone
to have a large rescue effect in a combined crisis**. An exact birth–death
counterexample maintains the elevated postlarval death hazard on every restored
recruit. At a 100-year horizon, extinction probability is .9758 with both
stresses and .0747 when recruitment alone is restored: a larval rescue effect
of .9011. A delayed-stage version gives the same qualitative counterexample.
These are deliberately constructed logical examples, not evidence that those
life histories existed. They refute the proposed general bound on R_L without
establishing the consultant's joint low-selectivity challenge region.

The supplied 17/28 versus 14/23 table gives a risk difference of −0.155 percentage
points, with a Wilson/Newcombe 95% interval of −25.12 to +25.35 points. Claude
is right about its low precision. The scanned source counts were not independently
accessible in this check; the interval was recalculated from the supplied counts.
Weak observed selectivity neither establishes biological equivalence nor
identifies the controlled larval-food effect.

**Retained target:** under independently constrained joint life histories, when
does restored recruitment cross a replacement boundary despite continuing
postlarval stress? Require fixed strategy distributions and joint covariance,
appropriate timing and viable life stages before testing weak selectivity.
If only freely chosen parameter combinations sustain the result, stop at the
logical finding. A large simulation is not yet justified. The three-observer
decomposition is useful for Q-038, but perfect-species truth, genus aggregation
and fossil observation must remain separate outcomes.

## 2. Chronology: test a consequential claim, not the existence of dependence

The [source/prior review](../literature/2026-10-21-chronology-dependency-prior-codex.md)
finds direct precedents for avoiding fossil-derived circularity: Arens & Jahren
(2002), Maloof et al. (2010, Cambrian), and Crampton et al. (2016). Schiffbauer
and Pulsipher already examine heterogeneous local SPICE expression and ordering.
A broad audit may be useful infrastructure, but the principle itself is occupied.

Farrell's supplement fixes biomere boundaries as common events in CONOP; SPICE
is separately located from isotope measurements. **That construction cannot
independently demonstrate interregional biomere synchrony, but it does not
automatically invalidate measured isotope–fossil order within a section.**
Numerical local lags additionally depend on age calibration. The consultation's
B2 distinguishes these targets better than Claude's summary does. No source
misconduct or invalid biological conclusion has been established.

The next useful product would identify at most three explicit mechanistic
claims and the temporal ordering or lag each requires. Trace the constraints
supporting those claims, including the ancestry of transferred ages. Determine
whether withholding target-derived links changes which mechanism remains
possible. A universal 0.5 Myr tolerance is not automatically meaningful for
different mechanisms. Losing resolution after ablation does not demonstrate
diachroneity. Without the raw CONOP input matrix, removing derived biozone ages
is an aggregate sensitivity analysis, not a clean test of the synchrony
constraint alone.

Retain that finite claim audit as a candidate. If it only rediscovers declared
assumptions or established local heterogeneity, fold its useful checks into
Q-041/Q-005 rather than promoting an audit to the programme's scientific target.

## 4. Innovation persistence is an attractive residual, not an unoccupied idea

[Rodland & Bottjer (2001)](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/sepm/palaios/article-abstract/16/1/95/99816/Biotic-Recovery-from-the-End-Permian-Mass?redirectedFrom=fulltext)
already describe temporary post-extinction expansion and subsequent restriction
of disaster taxa (abstract inspected). More decisively,
[Westrop et al. (2024, online 2023)](https://doi.org/10.1017/jpa.2023.75),
in the full-text orthid-shell-bed discussion and conclusions, explicitly contrast
transient Cambrian/early Ordovician orthid expansions with sustained Ordovician
success. They question simple competitive replacement and discuss environmental
and depositional alternatives. Thus the consultation's concrete contrast is
already an articulated research problem, not just an overlap with a generic
disaster-taxon category.

My inference is that the promising remainder is a *new discriminating design*:
among comparable ecological opportunities, which conditions permit persistence
after the transient opening closes? That needs an independently defined
capability/community state, a common persistence horizon, censored unsuccessful
episodes as well as eventual successes, and measured environmental opportunity.
An orthid bloom does not itself demonstrate the origin of a new capability.
Deprioritise as a separate entry until such a contrast and repeated support
exist; retain the connection to B-001/Q-009/Q-026. A new label for incumbent
recovery versus environmental tracking is not sufficient novelty.

## 6. What the consultation comparison can support

The repository-blind round supplies useful different topics. **It does not
isolate an effect of repository access, even on topic anchoring.** Topic window,
prompt, requested scale, simulation emphasis, output unit (six cards versus
three long briefs), and stochastic run all differ. The prompt also encodes
EPRP's standards. “More useful per word” is presently an evaluator judgment,
not a measured efficiency estimate. DOI resolution verifies bibliographic
identity, not claim accuracy or source-reading depth.

Retain the practical suggestion to use both blind ideation and informed
deepening, but mark it as a working hypothesis. A later method study could use
matched topics and identical prompts with repository access varied, replicate
runs, and blinded common evaluation. If the effect of the prompt's standards
