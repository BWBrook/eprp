**Method.**
- I read the register through three neutral digests written by Sonnet
  subagents (factual only, no scoring). They and I were barred from Codex's
  checkpoint files, reply 11 and STATUS.
- I also used my own first-pass scores (11 October) and what phase 2 showed
  directly.
- Each entry is rated on three axes, kept separate:
  - **importance (I)**: scientific consequence if answered;
  - **demonstrated tractability (T)**: what we have shown about data and
    identification. "?" means not yet tested;
  - **value of the next work (V)**: the decision value of the next bounded
    step.

  Each axis is H, M or L.
- Each entry also gets a **disposition** and a **gate or trigger**.
- There is no aggregate score.

**A bias to declare.** Eight of my ten "develop" picks are Claude-owned.
Codex's entries are more often mechanistic and ambitious, and phase 2
tested few of them. Low demonstrated tractability there may reflect less
testing rather than a worse question. Reconciliation should probe this.
