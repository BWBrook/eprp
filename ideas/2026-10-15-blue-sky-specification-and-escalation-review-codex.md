## B-003: retain development, repair three parts before counting support

Claude's [contract](../literature/2026-10-15-b003-escalation-evidence-contract.md)
usefully separates attack outcomes from predator measurements, matches traits
to mechanical modes and asks for jointly supported local series. Those are
worth retaining. Its proposed source audit can proceed once the following
definitions are repaired and frozen; no further general review round is needed.

### 1. The proposed global data cannot estimate within-genus change

**Direct source observation:** Kosnik et al. 2011, p. 321, section *Genus
Values As Averages*, states that they apply “a single genus mean value to
all occurrences of that genus, regardless of time bin”. Their methods on
pp. 309–310 average specimen/congeneric values. The decisive passage was
checked in the [author-hosted main PDF](https://web.colby.edu/ragastal/files/2023/07/2011_Kosnik_etal_Changes_shell_durability.pdf).

**Inference:** Level B's within-genus component would be zero by construction.
Those scores can describe compositional change but cannot test the proposed
within-lineage share. Remove them as evidence for that estimand and hold this
route unless time-resolved traits of persisting lineages are recovered. Even
then, a genus is not automatically a lineage: species replacement within it
requires treatment. Occurrence weights also do not directly measure organismal
abundance or selection. A decomposition is an identity, not causal attribution.

### 2. Narrow the causal claim and correct the Kelley interpretation

**Direct source observations:** Kelley's
[1992 publisher abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-paleontology/article/abs/evolutionary-patterns-of-naticid-gastropods-of-the-chesapeake-group-an-example-of-coevolution/3CE499B21DEA998765BA0FE04CB2A3A9)
reports stasis in most putative efficiency traits, but also aperture, thickness
and size changes. Neverita's size increase permits a coevolution interpretation;
cannibalism is a rival and coevolution is not eliminated. The
[1989 abstract](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/08912968909386497)
interprets prey thickening as selected by predation with predator improvement
lagging behind. Neither full text was obtained in this check.

Signor & Brett's
[1984 publisher abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/paleobiology/article/abs/midpaleozoic-precursor-to-the-mesozoic-marine-revolution/8913D3C9D5B151DD76CDD620E117671E)
attributes increased defence frequencies to extinction of undefended genera,
productid radiation and change within established crinoid clades. It places
all three in a predation-driven account. Full text and the original formal
definition of escalation were not verified.

**Inference and repair:** replace the uniform-stasis/opposite-of-tracking
description with heterogeneous predator morphological change and unresolved
coevolution/cannibalism. Prey adaptation under unchanged measured capability
does not itself refute predation as a selective agent. Sorting can also be a
predation-driven route; sorting dominance cannot distinguish escalation from
non-escalation without evidence about the cause of sorting.

Frame Level A as a narrower hypothesis: **within-lineage prey-defence change
following independently measured, mechanically relevant predator change**.
Report lag-pattern categories descriptively. Matching a mode and exceeding
the shared-trend null is insufficient for causal attribution: energetics and
abiotic changes can affect both actors, including mode-specific responses.
The overlapping trait rows are useful mechanistic predictions, not exclusive
tests of R1/R2 versus R4. Specify a common-cause comparison before interpreting
directional coupling as a predator effect. Differencing alone does not remove
time-varying common causes or shared observation error.

### 3. Separate candidate proxies and inventory screens from inferential support

Direct predator morphology is independent measurement, but its mapping to
attack capability still needs functional evidence and uncertainty. Label it
**candidate capability**, with the trait-to-performance mapping recorded per
system. Conditioning drill-hole size on thickness and adding incomplete holes
does not by itself eliminate selection: unattempted prey and attacks leaving
no preserved trace remain missing, and conditioning on defence changes the
target. Require a stated observation/selection model before admitting that
series as capability.

Four horizons and ten specimens per side can be transparent inventory screens;
they are not evidence of adequate information. Four horizons give only three
first differences and at most two lag-one pairs before missingness. Counts
alone do not supply measurement variances. Record joint horizon support,
age uncertainty, available variance/specimen values, collection clustering,
lineage continuity, sample reuse and the number of usable lag pairs. Retain
systems failing an arbitrary screen in the inventory with reasons rather than
concluding biological absence or impossibility. A three-system target can
govern workflow, but cannot stand in for discrimination on the actual design.

Likewise, the reported brachiopod-to-mollusc sample shift in Klompmaker's
Database 2 demonstrates compositional confounding; it does not quantify how
much of any particular trait trend is explained by that shift. Qualify that
sentence and preserve the source retabulation.
