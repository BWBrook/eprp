## What Claude changes, and what he does not establish

I accept the central concern: an apparent obstruction can be created by
restricting the growth programmes or the compensators. The original tranche
already required an enlarged-family/compensation challenge, but Claude makes
the reason for doing it first clearer. A certified all-stage bypass would be
a decisive and relatively cheap result for a specified comparison. I also
accept his Q-027 correction: the author-defined pairing screen passed three
pairs; the subsequent estimator check closed the selected contrast.

I do not adopt four stronger claims in the critique:

1. **Weak observed association does not establish independent evolution.**
   Our native-size calculations give linear R² values from 0.194 to 0.574,
   depending on ratio and size-pair sample. They describe selected morphological
   proxies. They do not identify independently adjustable developmental
   controls or a continuous route. Maturity, source selection and latent
   relationships remain relevant even to interpreting this descriptive gate.
2. **Full decoupling and one monotone coordinate do not guarantee a bypass.**
   A decoupled juvenile feasible set can itself be disconnected; a bounded
   monotone compensator may fail to reach a feasible connecting surface.
   Explicit compact polynomial counterexamples are in the
   [mathematical check](../data/b002-feasibility/mathematical-check.md).
3. **Failure to distinguish allometry from isometry is not equivalence.**
   Moreover, the held tables give chamber/septum volumes and endpoint component
   volumes, not the proposed wall/septum thickness series. Relabelling those
   data would fit the wrong physical quantity.
4. **Strict negative bottlenecks and path disconnection need not be equivalent.**
   The original strict-sign condition remains sufficient. Exact equivalence
   needs regularity assumptions; the zero-margin boundary is unresolved by
   component labels alone. The quasi-concavity exit is valid with convex joint
   allocation/domain conditions and admissible straight paths.

These corrections matter because the proposed rules could reject a mechanism
without testing it. They do not rescue B-002's current readiness.

## Completed bounded work

The revised tranche checked what the held sources support and calculated
the quantities they actually provide:

- **Workbook:** 178 of 179 series support the native 2→10 size comparison;
  124 support 5→20. Neither pair establishes juvenile/adult status. Derived
  ratios, exclusions and source-cell locators are retained. No coupling
  threshold or evolutionary interpretation is assigned to the regressions.
- **Volume proportions:** six selected late Fidelites chambers show a 63.67%
  decline in reconstructed septum/chamber volume ratio, already consistent
  with the source's allometry discussion. Printed rounding cannot explain
  that change, but reconstruction uncertainty and other changing components
  remain. This is not a thickness estimate or an observed buoyancy barrier.
- **Endpoint buoyancy:** the published component assumptions give required
  liquid fractions 0.3791, 0.3550 and 0.3132 for the three reconstructed
  endpoints. Source-discussed density alternatives retain feasibility within
  an idealised fully loadable chamber envelope. This replays an endpoint
  result; it does not evaluate earlier stages or physiological capacity.
- **Physical support:** earlier body chambers, soft-tissue occupation, wall
  material/displaced geometry and legitimate stage-specific loading rules
  are missing from the tables. Naglik's volume study explicitly did not
  measure body-chamber volumes. Stage q therefore cannot be calculated by
  combining the available columns, and there is no identified physical map
  on which to run the proposed component labelling.

Main Codex checked the source-table images and integrated separate GPT-6.1 Sol
mathematical and physical-source audits. Five interpolation controls passed;

the six regressions, and separate R arithmetic checked the physical diagnostics.
The source workbook and PDFs are unchanged. These are source and numerical
checks, not empirical validation of a new biological claim.
