# Working idea title

Status: sketch | Proposer: | Critic: | Updated:

Use roughly one or two pages when comparing candidates. Unknowns are useful;
mark them explicitly. The detailed fields matter before project promotion,
not before writing down a promising thought.

## Question and value

What is the biological or methodological question? What would answering it
change? If connecting deep time with modern biodiversity, specify what can be
transferred, at what scale, and where the analogy could fail.

## Closest prior work and proposed contribution

Sources/DOIs and exact relevant results; access depth; what has already been
done; the remaining contribution. Novelty status: **unverified** until checked.

## Target and evidence

Quantity/contrast to infer; response/predictors; population and inference scale;
sampling units; spatial, temporal and taxonomic resolution. Candidate resources
or theoretical model, access route, joint coverage and principal unknowns.

## Competing explanations and observation process

Biological mechanisms versus preservation, collection, publication and database
filters. Dating and taxonomic uncertainty; dependence and confounding. What
different mechanisms could produce the same apparent pattern?

## Discriminating test and smallest useful check

Predictions that differ between explanations; baseline/control; identification
assumptions; simulation or empirical validation route. Define what validation
is independent of. State an informative contrary/null result, what would make
us abandon or reformulate the idea, and rough effort/resources.

## Critique, disposition and handoff

Strongest objection and response; unresolved disagreement; recommendation and
next owner. If promoted, link Barry's decision, downstream repository, EPRP
commit and evidence snapshots. Separate the scientific prediction from criteria
for a valid and informative study.
