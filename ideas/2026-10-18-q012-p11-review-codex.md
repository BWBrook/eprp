With finite starting energy, the NUTS leapfrog check then flags a divergence;
a large finite energy error can also flag one without an exception.
The [CmdStan output documentation](https://mc-stan.org/docs/cmdstan-guide/mcmc_config.html#stan-csv-output-file)
likewise defines `divergent__` as a trajectory diagnostic.

The retained row is the valid state selected from the trajectory, **not the
failed proposal**. Its sigma=.79334 and rho=.10033 cannot rule out an excursion
near zero. Thus withdraw the stronger claim that the divergence did not occur
near zero; only the saved state is known. One chain-1 rejection and one
sampling divergence are compatible with a shared cause, but also with a
warmup rejection and an unrelated sampling divergence. The separate streams
cannot distinguish those explanations.

## Numerical amendment evidence

In the installed Stan Math implementation, `f_tol` is a post-solve residual
acceptance threshold. The Powell iterations use `rel_tol` and the iteration
limit; changing only `f_tol` does not improve or otherwise change the root.
The model's subsequent `check_tol=1e-8` checks do not prove that the lost
proposal would have passed its remaining guards or had accurate gradients.
Calling the original error spurious is therefore premature.

A bounded actual-Stan check of 108 P11 states covers 49 selected states,
the saved divergent state, each chain's minimum sigma, four starts, sigma
.01/.001 and 48 fixed stress states. Every point is accepted under both
1e-11 and 1e-10, with identical log density and all AD gradients at
15-digit output. This is consistent with the post-solve source semantics;
it is not an independent accuracy proof.

Two pre-existing broad-probe points discriminate the thresholds. Point 75
has residual 1.18234e-11 and is newly admitted at 1e-10 on R00 and after
mapping its latent vector to P11. Point 53, residual 2.61934e-10, still
fails both. Tightening `rel_tol` to 1e-14 and increasing the limit to 1,000
does not change those outcomes. Actual-LP finite differences at the newly
admitted P11 point agree with AD for log-sigma and kappa_b (scaled errors
below 3.6e-8 at step 1e-4).

The old fixed-innovation Simpson reference is unconverged at point 75,
whose exposures reach 1e8 and mode scales are .0063–.0093. It initially
reports log discrepancy .244 and very large derivative discrepancies;
changing its domain/resolution changes the answer substantially. This
is a reference failure, not demonstrated Q=64 failure.

A separate, chunked Simpson integration of the original kernel, centred
and scaled at independently Newton-computed modes, resolves those narrow
peaks. Doubling the local grid from 800 to 1,600 intervals and widening
the domain from +/-12 to +/-16 local scales leaves the log reference
unchanged and changes derivatives by at most 7.3e-8. Q=64 agrees within
1.5e-11 in value and 1.5e-7 in derivatives, well inside the existing
1e-4 / 1e-3 gates. This uses independent integration weights but shares
the model's algebraic kernel and mode/curvature formulas. Preserve both
the failed coarse reference and the converged check.

**Accept `solver_f_tol=1e-10` prospectively**, with `solver_rel_tol=1e-12`,
`solver_max_steps=200`, `check_tol=1e-8`, Q=64 and all model guards unchanged.
The finite evidence supports a bounded trial of that threshold. It does
not validate the unrecorded P11 proposal or guarantee a sampling repair.
