below, without turning this comparison into a new prior-work review.

There are **49 comparison cards** below. They are ordered in three bands,
with ties inside bands rather than 49 precise ordinal ranks. Band A contains
the allocation boundary, Band B consequential questions with potentially
valuable finite checks, and Band C retained questions whose next allocation
needs stronger justification or a specifically bounded diagnostic. Band C
does not mean low scientific importance. Inclusion is not an instruction to
start 49 investigations. The separate mapping below accounts for every Q/B
identifier, plus the later unnumbered developments; excluded questions remain
visible and can return when their stated obstacle changes.

Each card keeps three views separate:

1. **Consequence:** the account or interpretation changed by an informative
   positive or negative result. This is scientific judgment, not a measured score.
2. **Readiness:** target-specific prior work, separability, joint support and
   computation. Unknown means unknown; a download or a benchmark pass is not
   empirical readiness. Closest priors are reported at the access depth in
   the linked source records, not independently reread or validated here.
3. **Next work:** the cheapest check likely to change a disposition, with a
   route-specific exit. A cheap check can rank highly without its question
   earning sustained development. No weighted sum is used.

For every empirical row, retain source-specific permissions, dating,
taxonomic units and observation uncertainty. In particular, the
[PBDB hold](2026-10-23-response-closure-and-data-holds-codex.md) applies to its
named affected extracts. Author deposits have separate provenance; neither
automatic exclusion nor automatic public-record clearance follows from their
being PBDB-derived. No held empirical numbers become active evidence here.

## Why Q-027 gets this slot

**Q-027 versus Q-016.** Both could deliver a useful negative. Q-027 addresses
a sharper candidate disagreement about an actual biological pattern and has
a deeper audited support trail. But Benson already uses regional exclusion
and standardisation: repeating those controls is not the residual. The first
scientific task is to align Boag's molluscan/time-domain claim with the same
taxon, windows and richness contrast under regional control, and establish
whether that specific comparison is missing from the priors. An apparent
contradiction between unlike curves is not evidence that a question remains
open. Q-016 has a cleaner forecast estimand and may require
less reconstruction, but much of its broad forecasting territory is occupied
and recorded disappearance is an ambiguous extinction outcome. Q-027 wins for
this allocation by the combination of a named rival and investigated support,
not because its gate is passed. Q-016 wins under a preference for a narrowly
specified prospective prediction, conditional on a supported endpoint.

**Q-027 versus B-005.** B-005 has the strongest controlled mechanism design.
It could show that a diversity-dependence mechanism changes a statistic within
a simulator while Q-027 remains observational. Its current gap is more than
runtime: the statistic, observed domain and matched parameter/observation
contrast are not frozen. Benchmarking an implementation does not fill that
gap. Q-027 offers the shorter evidenced route to confronting an empirical
disagreement. B-005 wins if the protected output is explicitly a conditional
mechanistic prediction rather than a historical empirical comparison.

**Q-009 versus Q-027 and Q-016.** The new paired-site lead is scientifically
timely, but its present information describes community trajectories in
different settings. A source update during this ranking found and aligned
the original Pacific sample-level **percentage** workbook, beyond the
interpolated PANGAEA table. This materially improves its source readiness;
integer counts/denominators, blanks versus zeroes, the manuscript/workbook
sample-count discrepancy, investigator/time confounding and independent later
outcomes remain unresolved in the current source-owner record. The El Kef
unconformity and poorly constrained younger ages also remain. The small differences between two supplied
El Kef age columns do not encompass those uncertainties. Neither raw-sample
count nor a cross-site duration difference supplies independent later outcomes.
Q-009 could overtake the leaders if the source owner finds those outcomes and
valid holdouts. Its existing source tranche should finish; it does not need
the protected slot just to repeat that ownership.

**Q-040 versus Q-027.** Both connect instruments to interpretation. A
Q-040 result showing that two estimators imply different curves is useful but
can stop at sensitivity analysis. Without common support and occupancy it
does not resolve the historical rise. Q-027 supplies a more direct first
biological contrast. If the common-support reconstruction for Q-027 fails
while Q-040 has a defensible narrower contrast, the order can reverse.

**B-002 versus B-005.** Both allow a matched conditional theoretical result.
B-002 preserves greater developmental/morphological breadth and is not
disqualified by lacking immediate fossil transitions. Its model needs
programme-change rules, independently defensible physical inputs and more
precise novelty than “juveniles restrict adults.” B-005 currently has the
clearer mechanistic toggle and nearer spatial observation route. B-002 can
lead under a preference for theoretical morphology, after a successful worked
paper comparison; the growth workbook does not decide that comparison.

**Q-042 versus Q-040.** Q-042's headline is consequential and its conditional
bounds are already useful. What is missing is cohort information, rather than
a better richness estimator alone. Give it a narrow cohort/source check, not
the first sustained slot on the expectation that existing benchmarks made its
global inversion ready. Q-040 wins for an instrumental empirical application;
Q-042 wins if the desired output is a rigorous conditional-bounds result.

There is consequently **no objective-independent total winner**. Consequence
alone does not break the Q-027/B-005/Q-009 tie; readiness favours investigating
Q-027's specific gate and Q-016's support. Cheap-next-check value can instead
put Q-012's already authorised diagnostic ahead of either without granting it
the biological slot. A direct mechanism preference promotes B-005; a
forecasting preference promotes Q-016; a useful theory-only result can promote
B-002. These are real changes of scientific objective, not numerical weights
chosen to manufacture one rank.
