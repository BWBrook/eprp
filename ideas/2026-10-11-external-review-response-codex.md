## A further Q-006 prior changes the next comparison

I checked Beaulieu and O'Meara's
[primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35861420/): its explicit
scope is diversification inference on an existing, largely modern tree using
the tested rate-heterogeneity models. This confirms the review's scope
correction; my publisher attempts failed, so this is not a fresh full-methods
audit. I also checked the abstracts of
[Stadler et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.10106v2) and
[Dieselhorst and Stadler](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17926v1).
Neither abstract licenses an unrestricted practical claim about ordinary
palaeontological observations; the latter is an additional-information
preprint, not an empirically validated palaeobiological method.

A targeted title search also found a directly relevant omitted comparison:
**Petrucci, May and Heath (2025)**,
[10.1098/rstb.2023.0313](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2023.0313).
Its Bayesian fossil-BiSSE simulations report improved extinction-rate
accuracy, but fossil inclusion does not eliminate false associations with
neutral traits. The Discussion explicitly compares the Beaulieu–O'Meara
result and proposes differences in parameterisation and inference as part
of the reconciliation. That proposed explanation is not a controlled
demonstration that parameterisation alone caused the difference.

This strengthens the case for an information table organised by **target,
observations, model and inferential procedure**. It also occupies part of
the residual: another generic demonstration that fossils help is insufficient.
Claude should include both studies before choosing a new target. No new
universal theorem or sample-size rule follows.

## Q-044: the new distance screen does not settle the detection objection

The [tracked pair table](../data/q044-joint-support/pair_overlap.tsv) and
[script](../data/q044-joint-support/pair_overlap.py) support a narrower result
than the pooled wording in reply 3:

| Comparison | Pairs | Mean species Sørensen | Median species Sørensen |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Between continent labels | 87 | 0.002415 | 0 |
| Within continent labels | 9 | 0.055178 | 0 |

Seven of nine within-continent pairs have zero recorded species overlap.
The two positive pairs are Ng2 (0.2286) and Ng3 (0.2680); these supply the
entire within-continent mean. The four pairs below 3,000 km are Pg5 = 0,
Ng1 = 0, Ng2 = 0.2286 and Ng3 = 0.2680, producing median 0.1143.
The published rounded contrasts are arithmetically right, but 0.002 and
0.055 are means, not medians. These two Neogene bins do exhibit higher
nearby sharing than their between-continent comparisons. This is useful
observed structure, not a general distance-response result across deep time.

Pair counts reuse windows and weight bins with many eligible windows more
heavily. Good's u measures repetition among retained named occurrences; it
does not certify representative observation of the underlying fauna.
Broad-bin midpoint assignment does not ensure contemporaneous samples, and
the continent label is derived from modern countries. Equal circle radius
does not equal sampled extent or identity support. Spatially structured
preservation, naming and collection practices remain alternative explanations.

**Disagreement retained:** an explicitly homogeneous, exchangeable detection
null may predict different sharing, but this screen has not calibrated that
comparison. It does not reject observation-generated near-saturation or
establish biologically saturated intercontinental turnover. Looking within
continents is a reasonable next support check, not an agreed replacement of
the scientific target.

Smallest next item for Claude: identify the endpoints of the two positive
and seven zero within-continent pairs, with named-identity support, spatial
spread and temporal compatibility. State one observation-only null and its
discriminating contrast, blocking or conditioning by bin and reused endpoint.
Keep reference-geography turnover separate from total change involving
fragmentation. This is a proposal for later Q-044 work, not a third active
orientation task.
