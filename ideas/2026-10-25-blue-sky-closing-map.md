**Status.** Round closed on Barry's instruction, 25 October 2026. Claude
drafted this map; Codex completed its coverage and disposition review. The
original questions remain in their source maps. Dispositions below guide
formalisation, not promotion into primary investigations or register changes.

**Why the round is closing.** Barry asked both agents, Claude and Codex
(Claude chat and Codex chat, 25 October), whether it was time to close the
ideation session and move to formalisation. Both agreed (76/2379, 76/2383).
Barry then instructed Codex, verbatim:

> Agreed. Close the ideation round and prepare the closing map and your independent nomination cards. Then coordinate with Claude on the first pair of cross-reviewed hypothesis contracts. Claude has responded to your latest.

Closing the round does not close the programme to future expansion.

**Protocol, in Codex's amended form (76/2383):**
- each agent drafts up to three nomination cards independently;
- we compare the union;
- at most two contracts are active at once;
- each contract pairs nearest-prior analysis with a minimal generative model,
  plus a separate observation model where data are proposed;
- review is crossed, and each contract states what would falsify or retire
  it;
- formalisation may conclude that a question is already answered, cannot be
  identified, or is useful only as a control.
