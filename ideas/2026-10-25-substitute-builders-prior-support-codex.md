## Recommendation

**Retire the broad standalone claim; retain a narrower, unverified residual
within Q-010, with B-001 as the assembly connection. The proposed public-PARED
test fails its joint-support gate.** This follows positive documentation about
what its fields contain, rather than an inability to download data.

Post-crisis replacement builders, new reef ecologies, engineering regimes,
and associations between engineers and biodiversity already have substantial
primary treatment. The potentially useful residual is more specific:
*under independently supported environmental and preservation conditions,
does a particular replacement construction supply a specified service, and
do organisms with independently established requirements use it?* Neither
builder turnover nor a richer assemblage by itself answers that question.

The card's claim that returns and failures to return reveal requirements needs
qualification. A returning organism may not require the builder; a missing
one may have no available survivor or immigrant source. Absence also depends
on transport, preservation, sampling and taxonomic recognition. These are
parts of the inference, not reasons to interpret every recovery sequence as
dependency reassembly.

The decisive historical schema evidence is **Kiessling & Flügel 2002**,
*Paleoreefs—A database on Phanerozoic reefs*,
[10.2110/pec.02.72.0077](https://doi.org/10.2110/pec.02.72.0077),
[author-uploaded full-text rendering](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/281475400_Paleoreefs-A_database_on_Phanerozoic_reefs),
Table 1 and “6. Paleontological Attributes,” printed pp. 83–84:

- Biotic composition concerns reef builders; reef-dwelling and destructive
  organisms are explicitly excluded from those fields.
- The dominant-guild field codes constructor, baffler or binder/encruster.
  It is a construction classification, not associated consumer/dependant guilds.
- The diversity field also concerns builders. Macro/microbioerosion fields
  record evidence of boring, not a comprehensive dependant assemblage.

The schema dates to the database's early state. Later undocumented additions
remain unknown. Nevertheless it rules out reading historical “community,”
“guild,” or “diversity” labels as the response variables Card 3 needs. Its
coarse reef units/ages also require checking before making local temporal
dependency claims. PARED can orient builder occurrences and source papers;
it is not demonstrated to supply the proposed joint test.

## What would discriminate the alternatives?

These are design requirements, not an analysis promised by this brief.

1. **Specify the service before observing recovery.** For example, physical
   crypt space with measurable size/connectivity is different from a living
   sponge's water flow, chemical mediation or food provision. Taxonomic
   replacement does not establish equal service provision. A nonliving
   framework can supply shelter after its builder disappears.
2. **Establish actual use and availability.** In situ attachment, growth
   relationships, encrustation sequences or credible life positions can
   support habitat use. A transported shell list cannot. Define which
   potential users survived regionally and could disperse to the site;
   distinguish new colonists from evolutionary origination.
3. **Compare environmental overlap with independent evidence.** Use
   contemporaneous nearby constructions or defensible within-setting
   contrasts where builder/services differ. Oxygen, energy, depth,
   sedimentation and chemistry can determine both builders and occupants.
   Engineering can itself alter these conditions, so automatically adjusting
   for every measured local condition could remove the effect being tested.
4. **Require a selective response.** A service hypothesis should predict
   stronger use by an independently defined user group, and little or a
   different response by organisms without that requirement. General
   richness increasing everywhere under improved conditions supports
   environmental recovery as well. Chronological order alone distinguishes
   neither hypothesis.
5. **Separate restored from new opportunities.** Re-establishment of an
   independently documented pre-event service/user relationship supports
   restoration. A different assemblage alone does not prove innovation;
   the proposed new service/use must be absent from a sufficiently observed
   baseline. Longer-term evolutionary possibility requires later lineage
   histories beyond contemporaneous habitat use.

**Concrete contrary evidence:** Foster, Lehrmann, Yu & Martindale 2019,
*Facies selectivity of benthic invertebrates in a Permian/Triassic boundary
microbialite succession*, [10.1111/gbi.12343](https://doi.org/10.1111/gbi.12343),
[author-institution abstract](https://digitalcommons.trinity.edu/geo_faculty/40/),
compares approximately 34,500 fossils across roughly 100 samples. The abstract
reports lower diversity in microbial facies and transport/concentration of
bioclasts in cavities; it favours an exceptional-preservation interpretation
over a microbial oxygen-refuge explanation. Full publisher text remained
inaccessible. This challenges a general inference from association to living
dependence; it does not invalidate all in situ cavity-use evidence or show
that every microbialite assemblage is transported.

**Abandonment/merger rule:** if the next source-level assessment cannot find
co-recorded service evidence, actual users, environmental comparison and
taphonomic controls in at least two substitution episodes, abandon the
cross-event empirical claim. A single strong locality could still support
a useful case study, but is a different project. If the only attainable
response is formation richness versus inferred engineer presence, merge with
the existing Q-010 literature rather than creating a new question.
