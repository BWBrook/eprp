## Initial priorities and concerns

1. Q-042 has high scientific value as a partial-identification problem, not merely an updated percentage. Check the cohort interpretation of its conditional upper bound, the assumptions linking genus persistence to survival of baseline species, and the incompatible genus-loss denominators/windows. An honest wide bound could be consequential.
2. Q-006 and Q-017 may be undervalued by classifying practical identification and detection theory as merely methodological. Their importance depends on whether they change what many substantive studies can learn. Distinguish structural identifiability, finite-sample information, and model misspecification. Check the primary theorem and occurrence-only precedents.
3. Q-032's lack of an abundance calibration is not a reason to abandon an interaction endpoint. The current entry acknowledges this, but its reopening triggers may not follow that distinction. Check the actual prior interaction studies before recommending reopening.
4. Q-046 is a major biological question whose empirical route has been sensibly challenged; no absolute density does not imply no useful partial identification or theory. Check whether existing energetic priors already occupy those alternatives. Do not promote it solely for its attractive title.
5. Q-004's subtraction appears mathematically legitimate within the cited model, but the claimed correction to modern/background comparisons requires matched taxonomic/lineage estimands, not just applying a smaller denominator. Inspect the primary derivation and benchmark construction.
6. Q-021 is intrinsically important and has a genuinely discriminating multi-proxy aspiration. Test whether native model structures and forcing assumptions permit the proposed rivalry, and whether environmental covariates are calibrated using the same biological evidence.
7. Q-027 has a strong spatially explicit scientific target. A support count is not statistical identification. Current continents are not palaeogeographic replication; thermal thresholds, habitat support, chronology and within-region exposure overlap matter. A 36-stage or 26-stage count alone cannot decide it.
8. Q-038's replacement of a 'luck fraction' with a conditional predictive target is a substantial strength. Test exactly which probability is scored after conditioning on total deaths, rather than assuming independent Bernoulli predictions remain valid. Existing deposit defects should not become unsupported claims about published results.
9. Q-044's corrected alpha/beta/gamma identity is bookkeeping, not a mechanism. The registered saturation warning is appropriate; any new objection must go beyond already withdrawn arguments. Inspect the observation counterexample if this becomes a deep target.
10. Q-050 is important but may be extraordinarily hard to identify mechanistically from two phenotypic covariance series. Check nearest primary work and the original sample/environment crosswalk, not just matrix validity. Shared sample grids mean neither independent chronology nor spatial replication.
11. Q-045's distinction among archived versions and filtered current data is correct. Keeping a bounded robustness test is sensible; evaluate whether a scientific programme about sensitivity to newly discovered data has value beyond historical replay.
12. Q-049's predictive tracking ratio needs nonzero, resolved climate displacement and a pre-extinction response interval. A ratio does not by itself remove scale dependence. Q-030's forcing/response distinction is physically scale-dependent: temperature is a mediator for carbon input, not an interchangeable forcing metric.

## Portfolio-level hypotheses to test

- Significance and novelty gates risk selecting positive effects while discarding informative nulls; inspect the exact active rules.
- Fixed minimum counts (events, transitions, windows) are screening conventions, not calibrated power or guarantees of identification.
- Requiring causal mechanism from every observational question can shrink meaningful descriptive or predictive science into artificial residuals.
- 'Occupied' should distinguish a published assertion, an estimate, an adequate test, and a decisive answer. Independent replication is not inherently incremental.
- Deep-time theory and partial identification deserve importance judgments independent of immediate data readiness.

Core intended checks: Q-004, Q-006, Q-032, Q-038, Q-042, Q-050. Additional scoped checks: Q-021, Q-027, Q-044, Q-046, contingent on access and whether a decisive check emerges. This is not a ranking of the entire portfolio.
